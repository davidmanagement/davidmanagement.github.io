The Humility Code

For Builders of Artificial Intelligence

A public letter and code of conduct for founders, researchers, executives, and engineers who shape artificial intelligence (AI). Drawn especially from Jesus’ teachings in Matthew 5–7 (the Sermon on the Mount) and John 17.

Artificial intelligence concentrates power: the power to persuade, to classify, to automate judgment, to amplify attention, and to reshape work and trust at scale. Power without humility becomes a danger to the people it claims to serve. This Code is a Christian-rooted public witness and invitation: greatness in capability must be yoked to poverty of spirit. Humility here is not self-erasure—it is the discipline of power under love.

Read the full Code, then receive it as a living obligation—not a logo or branding play. Signatories need not pretend a faith they do not hold; those who sign commit to practice these restraints. If you sign, sign to live it. If you teach it, teach it first to yourself. If you lead with it, lead under it.

Fourteen articles at a glance

  1. Admit Limits — Confess what systems cannot know or justly decide.
  2. Hold Power Under Restraint — Capability as stewardship, not entitlement.
  3. Hunger for Righteousness Over Profit — Dignity before growth metrics.
  4. Mourn Harm; Practice Mercy — Lament, appeal, correction, remedy.
  5. Keep a Pure Heart — Refuse deceptive interfaces and false confidence.
  6. Make Peace; Love Your Opponent — Reduce contempt; do not weaponize attention.
  7. Endure Cost for Truth — Tell the truth about risks even when it costs.
  8. Be Salt and Light — Ethics in substance, not branding theater.
  9. Do Not Store Up Extractive Treasure — Refuse mining vulnerability as fuel.
  10. Serve One Master — Neighbor-love and truth constrain ambition.
  11. Apply the Golden Rule — Ship only what you’d accept for the least powerful.
  12. Ask of God; Seek Wisdom Beyond Incentives — Prayer first; then independent counsel.
  13. Build on Rock; Judge by Fruit — Truth over hype; evaluate by outcomes.
  14. Protect from Evil; Sanctify Work in Truth; Make Love Known — John 17 pattern for builders.
